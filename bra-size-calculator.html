      How to measure

      A soft tape measure is all you need. Stand straight with your arms down, and measure against your skin or over an unpadded bra.

      Underbust: the band does most of a bra's supporting, so this one is snug. Take the tape firmly around your ribcage, just under your bust, after breathing out.

      Bust: around the fullest part, with the tape lying flat and not pressing in.

      Bodies change. Measure again after a pregnancy, a change in weight, or when a bra that fitted stops fitting.

      Questions

      Why is my size different in different brands?
      Your size from your measurements is the same everywhere. Brands label it differently. Some use UK cup letters, where a US DDD is an E. Some sell letter sizes such as S, M and L, and their charts don't agree on which band and cup sizes each letter covers. Some don't make every band. Measured Size translates your size into each brand's own labels, and says so when a brand doesn't carry it.
      What is a sister size?
      The size one band up with one cup letter smaller, or one band down with one cup letter bigger. It holds the same cup volume on a looser or tighter band. Try one when your size is sold out, or when a band feels too tight (go up a band) or too loose (go down a band).
      Inches or centimeters?
      Either. Pick one with the switch above the boxes and type what the tape says, decimals included. Centimeters are turned into inches, to the hundredth, before anything is worked out, so the two give the same size.
      Does this page keep my measurements?
      Only if you tick "Remember my measurements", and then only in your browser on this phone or computer, until you choose "Forget my measurements". Your size is worked out on this page. Nothing you type is sent anywhere or put in the page's address, and the page loads nothing from anywhere else.
      Can I keep it on my phone?
      Yes. Add this page to your home screen and it opens like an app, and works with no connection. On Android, use the Add button under your results, or your browser's menu. On iPhone, tap Share in Safari (on iOS 26 and later, it is in the ⋯ menu), then Add to Home Screen.
