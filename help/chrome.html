Measured Size in Chrome on a computer

Measured Size is an extension: a small add-on that works inside Chrome. You add it once. Then, on any bra listing, one click shows your size in that store's own labels.

Add Measured Size to Chrome

Using Microsoft Edge or Brave? Follow the same steps there, from the same Chrome Web Store page. Edge asks first: click "Allow extensions from other stores".

  1. 1

    On your computer, open Measured Size on the Chrome Web Store.

    The Chrome Web Store is Google's shop for extensions. Measured Size is free. Reading this on your phone? Do these steps on your computer: go to measuredsize.com/help there, or search for "Measured Size" in the store.

  2. 2

    Click "Add to Chrome".

  3. 3

    Click "Add extension", the lighter button on the left.

    Chrome asks this for every extension. It says Measured Size can "Read and change your data on all gap.com sites and www.target.com": those two stores load their sizes as the page opens, so it is allowed to look there. Everywhere else it reads a page only when you click its button, and it sends nothing anywhere.

Put its button where you can see it

Chrome keeps a new extension behind the puzzle-piece button at the top right. Pinning Measured Size keeps its own button there, so it is one click away.

  1. 4

    Click the puzzle-piece button at the top right of Chrome.

  2. 5

    Click the pin, the small thumbtack, at the right of "Measured Size".

    Click the pin, not the name. Chrome also says "Access requested" here; you can leave that as it is.

  3. 6

    Find the Measured Size button: a pink square with an M, next to the puzzle piece.

    It stays there from now on.

Enter your measurements

  1. 7

    Click the Measured Size button.

    The first time, it asks for two measurements. You only do this once.

  2. 8

    Measure your underbust and your bust.

    Use a soft tape measure. Click "How do I measure?" in the box to see where, with a drawing for each.

  3. 9

    Type the two numbers in the "Underbust" and "Bust" boxes.

    If you measured in centimeters, click "cm" at the top of the box first. Decimals are fine.

  4. 10

    Click "Save measurements".

    It says "Measurements saved" for a moment. If you are already on a bra's page, your size shows straight away.

See your size on a bra listing

  1. 11

    Open a bra's own page in a store, like Macy's, Target or a brand's shop.

    A page for one bra, with its sizes, not a page of search results.

  2. 12

    Click the Measured Size button.

  3. 13

    Read your size.

    The big size is the one to choose on this listing, in the store's own labels. The rest of the box is explained on Reading the card. Click anywhere on the page to close it.

  4. 14

    Click "Show me on the page" to find your size on the store's page.

    The box closes, and Measured Size outlines your size on the page for a few seconds. It never picks a size for you.

  5. 15

    Look for the outline around your size on the page.

    The store may cross out a band until you pick the cup: that is the store's own page.

That's it. On every bra listing, click the Measured Size button.
