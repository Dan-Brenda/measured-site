Measured Size privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026. Applies to Measured Size version 1.0.1 and later for Chrome, and to the bra size calculator at measuredsize.com and the Measured Size fit API and MCP server (both below), which are separate from it.

In short

  • Your measurements stay on your device. Measured Size never sends them anywhere.
  • The extension has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • Measured Size does not sell or share your data, and has no affiliate links.
  • You can delete everything it saved at any time.
  • The bra size calculator at measuredsize.com stores nothing unless you tick "Remember my measurements", and then only on your phone or computer.
  • The fit API, for apps and AI agents, stores and logs nothing it is sent. It keeps only a count of requests per day.

What Measured Size is

Measured Size is a Chrome extension. You enter your underbust and bust measurements once. When you open a bra listing and click Measured Size's icon, it reads that listing and shows your size in the store's own labels, whether that size is in stock, and how sure it is.

What Measured Size saves, and where

Everything Measured Size saves is kept by Chrome on your device, in the extension's own storage. Nothing is saved anywhere else.

What Measured Size reads from web pages

Measured Size reads a page only when you click its icon, and only the tab you clicked it on. It reads what the listing shows: the product's name and brand, the sizes offered and which are in stock, the listing's size chart, and its description. To read a size chart, it may open the listing's own size guide and close it again.

Two stores are the exception, because their sizes and stock can't be read after the page has loaded:

  • Target (www.target.com): as a Target page loads, a small part of Measured Size listens for the stock answer Target's own page asks Target for, and copies each item's stock status into the page, where Measured Size reads it when you click. It sends nothing and asks Target for nothing.
  • Gap and Old Navy (gap.com sites): Measured Size's reader is on the page, waiting for your click, so it can read the size chart these stores open in a separate frame.

Measured Size never reads your measurements into a web page. They stay in the extension.

What leaves your device

Nothing Measured Size saves or reads is sent to Measured Size or to anyone else.

To answer, Measured Size sometimes asks the store you are on for more of that same listing's own information. These requests go only to that store (or, for Target's size chart, to Target's own chart page that the listing already links to), carry none of your data, and send no cookies:

  • On a Shopify store: the store's public product list for the listing, to tell sizes that are sold out from sizes that are not made.
  • On a few stores whose listing says so (today, Brooks): the store's own size-and-stock answer for the listing.
  • On Target: the listing's size chart page.

The store sees these requests as it sees the page's own: from your browser, for the listing you have open. They never say which size is yours.

Reporting a wrong size

"Report a wrong size" opens an email in your own email program, addressed to Measured Size, with the listing's address and the size Measured Size showed. Your measurements are added only if you tick "Add my measurements". You can read and change the email before sending it, and nothing is sent unless you send it. Measured Size uses an email you send only to fix its sizing, and does not share it.

No accounts, tracking, ads or selling

Measured Size has no sign-in and no account. It has no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no affiliate links. It does not sell, rent or share your data, does not use it for anything but showing your size, and does not use it to judge credit or lending.

Deleting your data

  • Change your measurements: open Measured Size and choose "Edit measurements".
  • Delete everything Measured Size saved: open Measured Size, choose "Edit measurements", then "Delete my saved data" and press it again to confirm.
  • Remove Measured Size: in Chrome, go to chrome://extensions and choose Remove. Chrome deletes everything Measured Size saved.

The bra size calculator (measuredsize.com)

The bra size calculator at measuredsize.com works out your size in your browser, on the page. It is separate from the extension, and the two share nothing.

  • Nothing you type leaves your phone or computer. The page sends nothing anywhere. It has no analytics, no cookies, no ads and nothing from another company.
  • It stores nothing unless you tick "Remember my measurements". Then it keeps your underbust and bust, and whether you entered inches or centimetres, in your browser on that phone or computer only, until you forget them.
  • One thing is kept without asking: if you close the hint to add the calculator to your home screen, your browser remembers that you closed it, so it isn't shown again. It is one setting, with nothing about you in it.
  • Added to your home screen, the calculator keeps a copy of its own files (the page, its scripts and its icons) on your phone, so it works with no connection. To install them and to update them, your phone fetches those files from measuredsize.com, and nothing else. These requests carry nothing you typed.
  • On iPhone, the app on your home screen has its own storage, separate from Safari's, so measurements remembered in one are not in the other. Safari may also clear what a website stored after 7 days of using Safari without visiting it; the home-screen app's storage is not cleared that way.
  • To forget your measurements: choose "Forget my measurements" on the calculator. Removing the app from your home screen, or clearing the site's data in your browser's settings, removes them too.
  • The host: the site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any website's host, GitHub handles each request's internet address to deliver the page, under its own privacy statement (docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The fit API and MCP server

The fit API (api.measuredsize.com) and its MCP server let apps and AI agents ask Measured Size's sizing engine for a size. They are separate from the extension: the extension never uses them and never sends them anything.

  • What it is sent: whatever the app or agent asks with: two measurements and a unit, a brand name, or a size and two brand names. No name, account or key is needed or asked for.
  • What it keeps: nothing it is sent. It works out the answer, sends it back and forgets the request. It writes no log of requests, and request logging on its host is switched off.
  • The one count it keeps: for each request, which endpoint or tool was used, whether it came over the API or MCP, and whether it was answered, refused, an error or over the limit. Nothing about who asked or what was asked. These counts are kept for three months.
  • Rate limiting: to stop abuse, each request's internet address is counted for one minute by the host's rate limiter. The address is not stored by Measured Size.
  • The host: the API runs on Cloudflare Workers. Like any network, Cloudflare handles each request's internet address and traffic information to deliver it, under its own privacy policy (cloudflare.com/privacypolicy).
  • Answers are not cached by browsers or by anything between, and the app or agent decides what it does with them.
