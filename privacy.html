Measured Size

Measured Size privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026. Applies to every way of using Measured Size: the browser extension for Chrome (version 1.0.1 and later) and for Firefox on computers and on Android; the Measured Size app for iPhone and iPad, with its Safari extension and its Shop tab; the bra size calculator at measuredsize.com; and the Measured Size fit API and MCP server.

In short

  • Your measurements stay on your device. Measured Size never sends them anywhere. On iPhone and iPad, the app and its Safari extension share them on the device.
  • The extension and the app have no accounts, no servers of their own, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • Measured Size does not sell or share your data, and has no affiliate links.
  • You can delete everything it saved at any time.
  • The app's Shop tab is a private browser: the stores' sign-ins, cookies and history last only until you close the app.
  • The stores you visit, in your browser or inside the app, have their own privacy policies, which Measured Size doesn't control.
  • The bra size calculator at measuredsize.com stores nothing unless you tick "Remember my measurements", and then only on your phone or computer.
  • The fit API, for apps and AI agents, stores and logs nothing it is sent. It keeps only a count of requests per day.

What Measured Size is

You enter your underbust and bust measurements once. On a bra listing, Measured Size reads that listing and shows your size in the store's own labels, whether that size is in stock, and how sure it is. There are three ways to use it, and each one has its own section below:

  • In Chrome or Firefox: a browser extension, on a computer, and in Firefox for Android.
  • On iPhone and iPad: the Measured Size app, which holds a Safari extension, a guide to switching it on, your size at each brand (My sizes), and a size calculator.
  • In the app's Shop tab: stores opened inside the app, read with the same extension.

The extension is the same code in every one of them, so what it saves, reads and sends is the same everywhere. Where it keeps what it saves, and how you delete it, depends on which way you use it.

What the extension reads from web pages

Measured Size reads a page only when you open it on that page (by clicking or tapping its icon), and only that page. The app's Shop tab is the exception: it reads each page you open there, below. It reads what the listing shows and the product data the page carries for it: the product's name and brand, the sizes offered and which are in stock, the listing's size chart, and its description. To read a size chart, it may open the listing's own size guide and close it again.

Two stores are the exception, because their sizes and stock can't be read after the page has loaded:

  • Target (www.target.com): as a Target page loads, a small part of Measured Size listens for the stock answer Target's own page asks Target for, and copies each item's stock status into the page, where Measured Size reads it when you open it. It sends nothing and asks Target for nothing.
  • Gap and Old Navy (gap.com sites): Measured Size's reader is on the page, waiting for you to open it, so it can read the size chart these stores open in a separate frame.

The size pill (on Target, in Safari on iPhone and iPad and in Firefox for Android). On a Target page, Measured Size looks at the page's address, and only its address, to tell whether it is a bra listing. If it is, and you have saved your measurements, it shows a small "Measured Size: find my size" button in a corner of the screen. Nothing on the page is read until you tap it. Tapping it opens the card over the page, which reads the listing just as opening Measured Size does; in Firefox for Android the card there reads nothing and says how to open Measured Size from Firefox's menu instead. To stay clear of the store's own buttons, it checks only what kind of element is under its corners, never any text. You can turn it off under More on the card.

Show me on the page. When you tap it on the card, Measured Size scrolls the listing to your size and outlines it for a few seconds. It never clicks, selects or changes anything on the page.

Measured Size never puts your measurements into a web page. They stay in the extension, and in the app.

What leaves your device

Nothing Measured Size saves or reads is sent to Measured Size or to anyone else.

To answer, Measured Size sometimes asks the store you are on for more of that same listing's own information. These requests go only to that store (or, for Target's size chart, to Target's own chart page that the listing already links to), carry none of your data, and send no cookies:

  • On a Shopify store: the store's public product list for the listing, to tell sizes that are sold out from sizes that are not made.
  • On a few stores whose listing says so (today, Brooks): the store's own size-and-stock answer for the listing.
  • On Target: the listing's size chart page.

The store sees these requests as it sees the page's own: from your browser or the app, for the listing you have open. They never say which size is yours.

Private and Incognito windows

Chrome, Firefox and Safari don't run Measured Size in a private or Incognito window unless you allow it in their settings. If you allow it, Measured Size reads listings there and shows your size as it does in a normal window, but nothing about what you do there is saved: not the listings it reads, not its short record of them, not the brands it did not recognise, not a size you choose (it lasts only while Measured Size is open), and not the rating tip. It uses the measurements you saved. If you save or change your measurements in a private window, they are kept, as from any window, because you asked for that; "Delete my saved data" works there too.

The stores you visit

The stores whose listings you open, in your browser or in the app's Shop tab, run their own sites with their own scripts and cookies, under their own privacy policies. Measured Size doesn't control what they collect, and receives nothing from them. It doesn't read their cookies, and it sends them nothing about you: only the requests for the listing's own information described above. (The Shop tab tells stores it is Safari, so they send it the pages they send Safari.)

Reporting a wrong size or a problem

"Report a wrong size" opens an email in your own email program (on iPhone and iPad, the Mail app), addressed to Measured Size, with the listing's address, the size Measured Size showed and your browser's name. Where Measured Size gives no size, "Report a problem" opens the same kind of email with the listing's address, your browser's name and what Measured Size said. Your measurements are added only if you tick "Add my measurements". You can read and change the email before sending it, and nothing is sent unless you send it. Measured Size uses an email you send only to fix its sizing, and does not share it.

No accounts, tracking, ads or selling

Measured Size has no sign-in and no account. It has no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no affiliate links. It does not sell, rent or share your data, does not use it for anything but showing your size, and does not use it to judge credit or lending. Where it links to another store, the link is the store's own address, with nothing added.

In Chrome and Firefox

Chrome or Firefox keeps what Measured Size saves on your computer or phone, in the extension's own storage, which no website can read.

  • Change your measurements: open Measured Size and choose "Edit measurements".
  • Delete everything Measured Size saved: open Measured Size, choose "Edit measurements", then "Delete my saved data" and press it again to confirm.
  • Remove Measured Size from Chrome: go to chrome://extensions and choose Remove. Chrome deletes everything Measured Size saved.
  • Remove Measured Size from Firefox: on a computer, open Add-ons and themes (about:addons) and choose Remove. In Firefox for Android, tap the menu (the three dots), then Extensions, then Manage extensions; choose Measured Size and tap Remove. Firefox deletes everything Measured Size saved.

On iPhone and iPad: the app and its Safari extension

The Safari extension. Safari keeps what the extension saves on your iPhone or iPad, in the extension's own storage, which no website can read. Safari asks you which websites the extension may read, and the two stores above (Target and Gap) are read as their pages load only on the sites you allow. Like other data on your device, what it saved may be in your iPhone's or iPad's own backup (to iCloud or a computer); Measured Size never receives it.

  • Change your measurements: open Measured Size in Safari and choose "Edit measurements". The app uses the new ones too.
  • Delete everything the Safari extension saved: open Measured Size in Safari, choose "Edit measurements", then "Delete my saved data" and press it again to confirm. This deletes what the app saved too.
  • Remove it: delete the Measured Size app, which removes its Safari extension too. To be sure nothing is left, use "Delete my saved data" first.

The rest of the app. The setup guide and the About screen keep nothing. The calculator works out your size inside the app, with no connection, and keeps nothing: what you type is gone when you close the app. Links in the app open in your browser or Mail, only when you tap them. On iOS 26.2 and later, the app asks iOS whether its own Safari extension is switched on, so it can show the right step; the answer stays on your device.

My sizes. It shows your size at each brand from the measurements the app keeps for the Shop tab (below): the same ones, in the same file, so what you save in either is in both. It works your sizes out inside the app, with no connection, and sends nothing. You can change your measurements there or on Home ("Update measurements"), or delete what the app saved ("Delete my saved data", then press it again to confirm).

Your measurements are shared between the Measured Size app and its Safari extension on your iPhone or iPad, and never leave it. Enter them once, in either: the other uses them too, and if you change them in both, the most recent save wins. They go through one small file that only the app and its own extension can reach (an App Group), encrypted by iOS while your device is locked and left out of its backups, with the time of that save. Deleting your saved data in either place deletes it in both: the shared file at once, and what the other one saved the next time you open it, before it shows anything. Safari and the app can't reach each other's own storage directly, which is why the other one deletes its copy itself.

The Shop tab

The Shop tab opens stores inside the app, and shows your size from the extension's card along the bottom of each bra listing.

  • A private browser. The stores' sign-ins, cookies, cache and history are kept in memory only, never written to your device, and are gone when the app closes (when you close it from the app switcher, or iOS closes it). While the app stays open in the background, they stay too.
  • What it reads. Each page you open in the Shop tab is read as it finishes loading, by the same extension, on your device: you opened the store in Measured Size to see your size. Nothing it reads leaves your device.
  • What it saves. Your measurements, and whether you have seen the rating tip, stay on your device in one file the app keeps, encrypted by iOS while your device is locked and left out of its backups. It also remembers, in a second small file kept the same way, the stores from its own list you starred, the six you opened most recently and the one you were last in (so the app can open there), whether you closed its Safari card, and whether you have seen its welcome and its three first-time tips: store names from the app's list, never a page's address. Everything else in the table above (the listings it checked, its short record, the brands it did not recognise, a size you chose) is kept in memory only, until the app closes, so the Shop tab keeps no history.
  • Delete it: on Home or in My sizes, choose "Update measurements", then "Delete my saved data" and press it again to confirm. This deletes the remembered stores too, and what the Safari extension saved. Deleting the app deletes all of it.
  • Open in Safari. It hands the page's address to iOS, which opens it in the store's own app if you have it, or else in your browser. Only the address goes; from then on, that app or browser and the store's own privacy policy apply. Buying happens there, not in the Shop tab.
  • Email and phone links on a store's page open Mail or Phone, only when you tap them.
  • The list of stores that work best in Safari is part of the app. Nothing is downloaded from Measured Size.

The bra size calculator (measuredsize.com)

The bra size calculator at measuredsize.com works out your size in your browser, on the page. It is separate from the extension, and the two share nothing.

  • Nothing you type leaves your phone or computer. The page sends nothing anywhere. It has no analytics, no cookies, no ads and nothing from another company.
  • It stores nothing unless you tick "Remember my measurements". Then it keeps your underbust and bust, and whether you entered inches or centimetres, in your browser on that phone or computer only, until you forget them.
  • One thing is kept without asking: if you close the hint to add the calculator to your home screen, your browser remembers that you closed it, so it isn't shown again. It is one setting, with nothing about you in it.
  • Added to your home screen, the calculator keeps a copy of its own files (the page, its scripts and its icons) on your phone, so it works with no connection. To install them and to update them, your phone fetches those files from measuredsize.com, and nothing else. These requests carry nothing you typed.
  • On iPhone, the app on your home screen has its own storage, separate from Safari's, so measurements remembered in one are not in the other. Safari may also clear what a website stored after 7 days of using Safari without visiting it; the home-screen app's storage is not cleared that way.
  • To forget your measurements: choose "Forget my measurements" on the calculator. Removing the app from your home screen, or clearing the site's data in your browser's settings, removes them too.
  • The host: the site is served by GitHub Pages. Like any website's host, GitHub handles each request's internet address to deliver the page, under its own privacy statement (docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The calculator in the Measured Size app is a copy of this one that keeps nothing (above).

The fit API and MCP server

The fit API (api.measuredsize.com) and its MCP server let apps and AI agents ask Measured Size's sizing engine for a size. They are separate from the extension and the app: neither uses them or sends them anything.

  • What it is sent: whatever the app or agent asks with: two measurements and a unit, a brand name, or a size and two brand names. No name, account or key is needed or asked for.
  • What it keeps: nothing it is sent. It works out the answer, sends it back and forgets the request. It writes no log of requests, and request logging on its host is switched off.
  • The one count it keeps: for each request, which endpoint or tool was used, whether it came over the API or MCP, and whether it was answered, refused, an error or over the limit. Nothing about who asked or what was asked. These counts are kept for three months.
  • Rate limiting: to stop abuse, each request's internet address is counted for one minute by the host's rate limiter. The address is not stored by Measured Size.
  • The host: the API runs on Cloudflare Workers. Like any network, Cloudflare handles each request's internet address and traffic information to deliver it, under its own privacy policy (cloudflare.com/privacypolicy).
  • Answers are not cached by browsers or by anything between, and the app or agent decides what it does with them.
