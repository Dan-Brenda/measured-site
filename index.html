Measured

Your bra size, in each store's own labels.

Measured is a free Chrome extension. Enter your underbust and bust once. On a bra listing, click its icon to see your size as that listing labels it (34DD, 34E, 85E, M, S (D-DD) and so on), whether it is in stock, and how sure Measured is.

  • When your size is sold out, it can show a true sister size one band away, and says so.
  • When nothing here fits, it says "Not available here" and names the closest size in stock, rather than guessing.
  • Your measurements stay on your device. No account, no tracking, no ads.

Where it works best

Amazon, Walmart, Target, Macy's, Nordstrom, Kohl's and JCPenney, and many brands' own stores, including Shopify stores. On other sites it reads what it can, and says plainly when it can't.

Privacy

Everything Measured saves stays in your browser, and nothing is sent to Measured. Read the privacy policy.
