What Measured Size is

Measured Size works out a bra size from two measurements, underbust and bust, and gives it in each brand's own labels, with how sure it is. It comes in four parts:

  • The Chrome extension, which reads the bra listing you have open and shows your size in that listing's labels, whether it is in stock, and how sure it is.
  • The bra size calculator at measuredsize.com, which works out your size on the page.
  • The fit API at api.measuredsize.com, for apps.
  • The MCP server at api.measuredsize.com/mcp, for AI agents.

All four are free. "You" in these terms means anyone who uses any of them, including an app or agent and whoever runs it. By using Measured Size you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use it.

Sizes are guidance, not a guarantee of fit

  • A size from Measured Size is its best reading of your measurements and the brand's published sizing. It is not a promise that a bra will fit you.
  • It doesn't judge your shape, a style's cut, or how you like a bra to feel. Two people with the same measurements can wear different sizes.
  • Brands and listings can differ from their own charts, and charts change. A style, a listing or a store's stock can differ from what the brand publishes, and Measured Size can read a listing wrongly.
  • What the extension says about stock is what the listing showed when it was read, and stock can change.
  • Every answer says how sure Measured Size is, and some answers are refusals: where it can't size you honestly, it says so rather than guess. Keep the confidence, and any refusal, with the size.
  • Check the brand's own size chart and the store's returns policy before you buy. What you buy, and any return, exchange or refund, is between you and the store.

Not medical advice

Measured Size uses body measurements only to work out a clothing size. It is not medical advice, and it doesn't diagnose or treat anything. For pain, a change in your breasts, or a bra after surgery, ask a doctor or another health professional.
